# 🩺 Pulse

Service health, regenerated by the `service-health` job. Each service has its own block and its own 🕐 line: a stale line means the job did not refresh that service. Back to [[cockpit]].

Services come from [[services]]. A service with no block, or whose block says "not verified", is a coverage gap, not a healthy service.
